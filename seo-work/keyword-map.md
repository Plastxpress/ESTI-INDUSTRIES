## Two tracks

- **Nearer-term (long-tail):** product + spec queries ("5 ply corrugated box", "octabin 1000 kg",
  "UN 1266 box drop test"). Supported now by product titles, spec tables, Product `additionalProperty`
  and per-product FAQs.
- **Head-term leadership:** "corrugated box manufacturer in India / Maharashtra". The home page owns it.
  Ranking for this needs off-site work too (Google Business Profile, directory listings, links) — see
  delivery-summary.md. On-site work alone will not win a competitive head term.
